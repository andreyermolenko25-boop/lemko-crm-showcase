Features and integrations

An automation is a short sentence: when something happens, if conditions match, do these actions.

  • Triggers: a record is created, a field changes (optionally to a specific value), a schedule (every minute, hour, day, week or month at a set time in your time zone), or an incoming webhook from another system.
  • Conditions: compare a field — is, is not, greater than, less than, contains, is empty.
  • Actions: create a record, update a field, notify a colleague, send an e-mail, assign an owner, or call an outgoing webhook.

Text can include fields from the record. The activity feed shows every run in plain language, a failed action never undoes the change your colleague made, and automations do not trigger themselves in a loop.

Connect any mailbox over IMAP/SMTP — Gmail, Outlook or your own mail server (use an app password where your provider requires one).

  • New mail is fetched about every two minutes, including your Sent folder, so replies written in Gmail appear too. The first sync pulls the last 14 days.
  • Messages are threaded and attached to the contact by e-mail address; unknown senders can become new contacts automatically.
  • Replies sent from Lemko go out through your own mailbox and are saved to its Sent folder.
  • The mailbox password is stored encrypted, and connections are made only to public servers.

Shared mailboxes such as info@ or support@ work alongside personal ones.

Clients book on a public booking page or a widget embedded on your site. Free slots are calculated from staff shifts minus existing appointments, respecting the minimum notice and how far ahead you allow.

Payments go to you. You connect your own Stripe account, the client pays when booking, and the money goes directly to your account. Lemko takes no commission — Stripe's normal processing fees apply on your Stripe account.

  • An unpaid booking holds the slot for 30 minutes and is then released automatically.
  • Payment is confirmed by asking Stripe directly, never by trusting the browser address bar.
  • Clients get reminders by e-mail and, where SMS is enabled, by text message 24 hours and 2 hours before the visit, and can cancel through a personal link without signing in.

Security and your data

Protection is on by default and you can tighten it for the whole team:

  • Sign-in: passwords are stored as Argon2id hashes; authenticator app, SMS code, passkeys and backup codes for a second factor; sign-in with Google, Microsoft or Apple.
  • Workspace policy: require two-factor sign-in for everyone, choose the allowed sign-in methods, and set an idle timeout, a minimum password length, an IP allow-list and new-device alerts.
  • Sessions: see every active device and sign it out remotely; changing your password ends other sessions.
  • Secrets: API keys, mailbox passwords and webhook secrets are encrypted at rest.
  • Accountability: an audit trail records who changed what, when and from which address, and deleted people and clients can be restored.
  • Abuse protection: rate limiting on sign-in, and outbound connections to private networks are blocked.

The measures are listed in full in Annex 2 of the Data Processing Agreement.

You do. For the data you put into Lemko CRM you are the controller and we are your processor — we use it only to run the service for you.

  • Export any list to CSV, all records to JSON, and your workspace configuration (views, dashboards, reports, automations) to JSON.
  • After a subscription ends you have 30 days to export; then the data is deleted from production and backups are overwritten within 35 days.
  • If you need a full structured export, write to us and we will help.

Lemko Automation is a Polish provider subject to the GDPR and the Polish Personal Data Protection Act. Our [Data Processing Agreement](/en/dpa) under Art. 28 GDPR is part of the Terms and applies automatically — no separate signature, and a signed copy on request.

It covers instructions, confidentiality, security measures, sub-processors with 30 days' notice of changes, breach notification within 48 hours, help with data-subject requests, audits, and deletion at the end.

Lemko also helps with your own duties: the reports catalogue includes ready templates for a record of processing activities (Art. 30), a reply to a data-subject access request (Art. 15), an incident report (Arts. 33–34) and a complaints register, and web forms can carry a consent checkbox.

The hosting provider and its location are named in Annex 3 of the DPA, together with every other sub-processor — for example e-mail and SMS delivery.

We aim to keep processing inside the European Economic Area. If a provider is outside it, the transfer relies on an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. We never sell data or use it for advertising, and we give 30 days' notice before adding a sub-processor so you can object.

Technically yes — clinics use notes and attachments, including medical images. But you decide what goes in, and you are responsible for it: special categories of data (Art. 9 GDPR) need their own legal basis.

To reduce risk, enter only what you need, use roles so only the right people see it, and enforce two-factor sign-in for the workspace.

Plans and billing

Anytime. Adding seats and moving to a higher plan apply immediately and you pay the proportional amount for the rest of the period; removing seats or moving to a lower plan takes effect from the next billing period.

Seat limits per plan are shown on the pricing cards. A yearly subscription costs ten months — two months free.

Billing runs through Stripe: a subscription per seat, monthly or yearly, in euro, charged in advance.

  • Prices are net. VAT is added where it applies. Add your EU VAT number at checkout; businesses in other EU countries with a valid number are invoiced under the reverse-charge mechanism.
  • Invoices are electronic and sent by e-mail; where Polish law requires it they are issued through KSeF.
  • Payment methods are the ones Stripe offers for subscriptions in your country, and promo codes can be entered at checkout.

Cancel anytime — the plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

  • Cancel during the trial and you are never charged.
  • Fees for the unused part of a paid period are not refunded, except where the law requires it — for example a consumer's 14-day right of withdrawal.
  • After you cancel you have 30 days to export your data.

We tell the account owner by e-mail and Stripe retries the charge. If the amount stays unpaid for 7 days, access to the workspace can be restricted until you pay — your data is not deleted. After 30 days unpaid we may end the agreement, and the 30-day export period applies.
